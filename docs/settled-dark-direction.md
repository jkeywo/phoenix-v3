# Settled Dark: agreed Phoenix direction

Source: final review summary in Codex thread `01a13b70-2aef-7453-93fe-453c47eecd7c`, “Explore Settled Dark setting pivot”. Recovered 17 October 2026. This later summary supersedes the initial suggestion of several centuries before the conquest. It records the prior agreement; it is an input to the V3 rewrite, not proof of implementation.

+We’ve completed the review of all 18 issues. **Phoenix now has an agreed Settled Dark direction**, with the following additions to the setting.

| Area | Agreed direction |
|---|---|
| **Historical period** | Immediately before the Ascendant’s conquest. Uneven decline combines fractured authority, failing infrastructure and an exhausted social order. |
| **Player impact** | Crews save people and provide temporary relief. Decline appears through concrete authored pressures, not automatic decay that undoes successes. |
| **The conquest** | Outside the opening frame. It enters play only through an explicitly authored campaign or GM. |
| **Starting region** | An original district on the settled frontier, with a principal hub, smaller ports and routes into lost human connections and genuinely unknown space. |
| **Player organisation** | Professional household service. Starter crews serve **Caelifen** under broad standing orders; other campaigns can choose another House. |
| **Political geography** | Caelifen governs the hub; surrounding jurisdictions vary. Alliances and enemies are campaign-defined. Human opposition predominates, with rare ancient or non-human threats. |
| **Tone** | Worthwhile work with selected hard compromises. Occasionally overt horror, restrained depictions of suffering, and religion mainly as institutional background. |
| **Interstellar passage** | An attached Guild navigator enables passage. Crews can assess and attempt uncharted routes. Routine journeys are compressed; occasional local disturbances supplement practical route failures. |
| **Local and formation jumps** | Local jumps are specialist fittings, including on the starter Cruiser. Each formation member needs its own working drive and navigator. |
| **Displacement** | Transfers a bounded volume containing a small team or compact load over short range. Reliable within stated limits; sufficient destination knowledge is required, but no universal receiving pad. |
| **Navigator capacity** | One active passage operation at a time across interstellar transit, local jumps and displacement. Preparation may overlap. |
| **Shields** | Engineered defensive fields. Tuning remains as measured field response. Shield sectors obstructing displacement must be lowered. |
| **Tractors and support** | Engineered force projectors can influence shielded vessels within force limits. Compatible remote energy and shield assistance remain; physical repairs require teams or work craft. |
| **Weapons** | Kinetics and missiles dominate, with specialist beams. Deep-based destructive weapons are rare. Houses share engineering principles but have distinct designs and doctrines. |
| **Sensing and mysteries** | Psychic perception concerns conditions of the deep. Suitable conventional scans can sometimes establish interior displacement destinations. Investigations yield useful local answers while larger cosmological questions remain unresolved. |
| **Communications and psychic warfare** | Interstellar messages travel aboard ships. **The entire EWar family becomes psychic attack and defence**, producing real effects in signals and equipment. House-trained specialists require fitted apparatus; they are separate from navigators. |
| **Crew and machines** | Delegation represents watch personnel and bounded automation. Genuine machine minds are exceptional and difficult to reproduce. |
| **Engineering and supply** | Reaction engines with abstracted manoeuvring propellant. Ordinary technology is understood but materially constrained. Workshops make consumables and common parts; specialised components require supply or salvage. Home-base replenishment uses House requisition and actual stocks. |
| **Personnel and medicine** | Restorative prosthetics and selected enhancements are familiar. Medicine treats minor injuries and stabilises severe casualties. Ships can carry qualified relief personnel where authored. |
| **Ships and presentation** | Courier complement: **dozens**. Cruiser complement: **thousands**. Veteran, maintained industrial vessels with House heraldry. Consoles use **retro vector holographic** styling. |
| **Introductory missions** | Three linked, independently playable missions follow **a frontier route under strain**: routine work, assistance, then a contested operation involving rival-backed privateers. Include one bounded excursion beyond reliable charts. Both hulls retain appropriate opportunities to learn. |

The implementation principle remains **theme and modest mechanical differences expressed through authored data**. Where the completed redesign cannot express an agreed variation, extend the shared mechanism.

The main flexibility checks are:

- Qualified personnel requirements, relief coverage and shared specialist capacity.
- Equipment interactions with shield sectors and recipient compatibility.
- Psychic attack/defence profiles, presentation and bounded effects.
- Route knowledge, uncertain passage, compressed journeys and ship-carried information.
- Production recipes, requisition access and aggregate personnel scale.

These are **audit candidates, not confirmed engine deficiencies**. The completed redesign still needs checking against them. Detailed scenario timing also remains unvalidated: the required design digest was blocked by Python interpreter access.
