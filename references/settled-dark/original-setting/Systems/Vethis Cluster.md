## Vethis Secundus
*Pacified Territory · Imperial (Campaign Pale active)*

An industrial world of the first order — population eight billion, fifty generations of continuous settlement. The Janissariat brought Campaign Pale here fourteen years ago. It is not resolved.

The population's resistance has no organised military structure, no identifiable command, no central node to target. What the Janissariat has encountered is a population that spent fifty generations developing sophisticated collective non-cooperation: administrative records unavailable when requested, industrial equipment that functions for the population and malfunctions for imposed production schedules, a communication pattern the signals division has been attempting to map for eleven months without a satisfactory model.

The parallel governance structure is not led. It is *practiced*.

**Language:** Vethik — fifty generations removed from its source languages. The Janissariat's translation capacity is poor.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Output, Order

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Underground Sites
> The underground sites destroyed in the sixth month of Campaign Pale predate human settlement by an interval the geological survey declined to specify. They showed signs of recent use — not by the local population, who were genuinely surprised.
>
> What J3 saw when the sites were breached and has told no one: the sites were not storage. They were functional — active at a low level, doing something. The breach interrupted that function. What it attracted was something the interruption drew to the surface. Three soldiers went into it and are listed as KIA. This is not what happened to them.
>
> The population has a tradition, passed through community-organising networks for generations, about what lies beneath the industrial zones. It is not a ghost story. It is a set of practical instructions: how to behave, what not to do, how to act when the ground becomes active. These instructions predate the Conclave. Some elders believe they predate human settlement entirely.
>
> The population is not waiting in fear. They are waiting with the specific patience of people who have always known something was coming and have been told what to do when it does.
>
> Cross-reference: [[Pre-Human Presence Thread]], [[Fraying Conditioning Thread]] (the three soldiers at [[Irongate]]).

## Vethis Prime
*Industrial World · Imperial*

Vethis Secundus has a sister. Population six billion. Where Secundus's resistance culture is built around non-cooperation and parallel governance, Prime's has developed through industrial organisation — collective work practices, production-pace coordination. Two different challenges that have learned to coordinate.

The Janissariat garrison on Vethis Prime falls under a different command structure than Campaign Pale — an administrative irregularity the two garrisons share no operational intelligence across. Ae1 has noted it.

Two months ago, non-cooperation practices from the Vethis Secundus model began appearing in Vethis Prime's industrial sector. The garrison commander has sent three requests for guidance. None have received a response.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Output

## Other Vethis Cluster Locations

**[[The Narrow]]** — Strategic corridor between two dense stellar formations. Single viable navigation route between inner systems and southern rim. The waystation council of six has been running affairs for thirty generations; they paid the Conclave and will pay the Ascendant. Their patience for political arrangements is significant.

**[[Pale Coast]]** — Agriculturally productive world. Two Ecclesiatum prelates, posted independently to different settlements, have been independently reaching the same conclusion about legitimate resistance theology — through entirely different routes, without knowledge of each other.

**[[Embrek]]** — Fringe world of exiles and inconvenient people; the most functionally egalitarian community in the Settled Dark. The governor has been in post twenty-two years. Construction is happening on the planet's surface under the guise of a communications relay. Four days ago a halt was called; a decision required communication with the project's unknown commissioner, who took four days to respond.

**[[The Outer Tongue]]** — Last waystation before the uncharted void. Crews returning from the outer void have been describing, for three months, a sense of *approach* at a specific interval outside the charted edge. Three crews have turned back from it. None have filed reports with the Guild.

**[[Cinder Rest]]** — Thirty-generation outer void habitat. The oldest resident spent forty years keeping an observational log describing *the outer dark developing a preference* — a directionality first noticed thirty years into her practice. The current watch-keeper has been noticing the quality intensifying for seven months and has not yet found the words to write it down.

**[[Outfall]]** — Extreme outer research station beyond the Guild's operational charts. Three months ago, instruments began detecting a structure at extreme void distance — not a stellar object, not a navigational hazard. Something becoming visible. The researcher has a draft paper that no journal will accept.
