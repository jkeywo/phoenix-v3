## Caelifen
*Noble House Throne World · House Caelifen*

Population five billion, roughly half employed in some aspect of trade: orbital docking complexes handling more void-freight than any location outside the Seam, financial institutions, brokerage firms, and the enormous service economy behind all of it. The Caelifen family is good at exchange.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Output, Order

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Anomalous Cargo
> Navigation instruments of unrecognised design arrived at Caelifen's outer docking complex six months ago and have been in a secure warehouse since. Two technical staff attempted to determine what they are calibrated for. Both requested reassignment. Both were transferred without question. The instruments are still there.
>
> One former staff member did not leave Caelifen. She is currently employed by a minor commercial research firm and working on the instrument question on her own time. She is close. If C3 knew she was still working on it, they would find this worth managing.

## Ondrak's Reach
*Noble House Throne World · House Ondrak*

A fortified world built with pragmatic attention to defensibility. The main settlement is positioned in terrain offering natural advantage. The orbital infrastructure includes capabilities the conquest-era agreements nominally prohibit and that no one has formally inventoried. The household troops are widely considered the best-trained private military among the surviving Houses.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Output, Order

---

## Vorth Station
*Commercial Hub · House Caelifen / House Ondrak (joint)*

A station at the intersection of three void-trade routes. Caelifen acquired it early; House Ondrak acquired 37% of dock capacity during the conquest's disruption period in a transaction whose circumstances are not discussed. Nine million permanent residents. The food is extraordinary.

Houses a small community of ex-Ductus navigators — washed-out trainees doing commercial route calculation. Two of them have been experiencing *the thing that got me washed out, but clearer now* for the past four months.

**Statistics:** Output, Order

## Other Arm Locations

**[[The Intermediary]]** — Legally independent waystation at the junction of Seam approach lanes and Caelifen arm commercial traffic. A legal research paper commissioned through a broker here has produced detailed analysis of the strongest available legal arguments for territorial self-determination. The research was commissioned by V1's legal staff. The researchers don't know this.

**[[Meridion]]** — Caelifen's outer commercial node. The senior freight broker noticed a manifest irregularity on C3's anomalous cargo — the technical classification code changed between the second and third passage — and has kept a private copy.

**[[The Lumen]]** — Caelifen's outermost commercial node at the fringe. One of C3's instrument shipments entered the Caelifen commercial network here through a fringe prospecting vessel with no obvious reason to be carrying precision navigation equipment.

**[[Orvath]]** — Ondrak's outer fortified post. O3's audit will, within thirty days, find a contract containing clauses giving Caelifen acquisition rights over Ondrak commercial assets in this arm. The administrator who originally negotiated the contract was chosen for their lack of specialisation in commercial law.

**[[Pale Hook]]** — Ondrak's outer military staging station. Significantly over-capacity for its current population of 40,000. The commander was transferred here eight months ago, at the same time as defensive systems were updated, with contingent orders phrased as *upon receipt of activation*.

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — Pale Hook Orders
> The contingent orders direct rapid deployment toward [[New Sorenne]], [[Sorenne's Edge]], and the shipping lanes between them. The orders contain no political framing. The commander is professional enough to execute them without it. O1 has been counting on this.
