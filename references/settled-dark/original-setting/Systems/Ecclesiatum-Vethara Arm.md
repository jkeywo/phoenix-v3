## Tessavar
*Noble House Throne World · House Vethara*

A world of considerable beauty and limited agricultural viability. House Vethara's institutional memory reaches to the first Conclave settlements. The archive at Tessavar contains what the House claims is the most complete account of the Settled Dark's settlement history available anywhere.

The Ecclesiatum presence here is substantial and complex. The Holy World declaration — which House Vethara resisted for decades — was accepted four hundred years ago on terms that are not public.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Output, Faith

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Forgery
> The terms of the Holy World negotiation included, in part, the introduction of documentation that would not need to be used immediately. The forgery underpinning V1's Charter claim dates from this period. V1's predecessor placed it. The terms are not public.

## Tessavar Minor
*Holy World · Ecclesiatum*

Chosen for *spiritual resonance*. The primary spiritual complex is vast, built over millennia of accumulated Ecclesiatum construction onto an older foundation whose original architecture is partially visible in the complex's lower levels. The Ecclesiatum documents the older structure as pre-settlement indigenous construction.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Faith

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Older Foundation
> The site produces a consistent, measurable effect on those with perceptual sensitivity — a clarity of reception that the contemplative community has been cultivating, and that the site apparently amplifies.
>
> E4's investigation has produced an unfiled finding: the quality of perception available at Tessavar Minor matches, in character if not intensity, what the Vethis Secundus population described as being active beneath their world. E4's working question is whether the foundations here and on Vethis are related in origin.
>
> Cross-reference: [[Pre-Human Presence Thread]].

## Orreval
*Holy World · Ecclesiatum*

Settled before the Ecclesiatum existed in its current form. The resident community predates the institutional Ecclesiatum and accepted formalisation with the tolerance of people who were already doing the thing being recognised.

The community's continuous records extend to the original settlement. The first three generations of community leaders recorded *the attending* — a quality of presence at this world that is directional. Not localised to the planet. Pointing somewhere.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Faith

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Attending
> This language predates the Schola Perpetua's vocabulary by approximately a millennium. E1 does not know these records exist. The community has never hidden them, but they are in Orreval Speech and have never been systematically catalogued. The community's current record-keeper has been considering for a decade whether the current moment is the correct time to make them known.

## Vethara Seat
*Noble House Administrative World · House Vethara*

The operational headquarters of House Vethara's commercial and territorial operations. Dense with archivists, lawyers, territorial administrators. Culture of careful formal precision.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Faith, Order

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — Survey Findings
> V2's survey commission found structural remains in the outer administrative zone of the same category as those at [[Pale Meridian]] and the Seam's Underbelly. V2 logged them as geological formations and has been postponing telling V1 for six weeks. Cross-reference: [[Pre-Human Presence Thread]].

## Other Arm Locations

**[[The Scar]]** — A region of the void where immaterial navigation fails. Guild navigators describe it as *an absence* — not disrupted current, but void where current should be, as though something has been removed. The Scar is not natural.

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Scar's Origin
> The research community's most recent published work proposes a navigational accident as the mechanism. The researchers who proposed it know it is wrong — the mathematics require an energy source four orders of magnitude larger than any known Ductus vessel. The correct answer, which the mathematics also allow: the Scar was made *deliberately*, by something capable of removing the immaterial current from a region of space. They proposed the accident because publishing the correct answer had consequences none of them were prepared for.

**[[The Cartouche]]** — Vethara's outer survey station. Six hundred years of survey archive. The head researcher has been cross-referencing structural catalogues against canyon inscription data from Tessavar Minor's ancient foundation. Three weeks ago found a structural type consistent with the Seam's Underbelly architecture.

**[[Keth's Anchorage]]** — Independent settlement governing under a developed colonial charter for sixty years. Their jurisprudence has independently produced the strongest available legal precedent for territorial self-determination. V1's legal staff have been quietly using these documents in the Charter negotiations for three months without the community's knowledge.
