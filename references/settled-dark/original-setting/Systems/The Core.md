## The Seam
*Imperial Capital · The Ascendant*

The name is a Guild navigation term for the immaterial convergence at the system's centre. Transit is easiest here; Guild vessels can arrive and depart along more headings than anywhere else. The Ascendant took the name and the system both.

The administrative population numbers approximately forty million. The Ductus Guild maintains its most significant non-Anchorage presence here. All Noble Houses maintain representation. The Ecclesiatum's mission is headed by E1 personally.

The Seam has a specific quality visitors notice but rarely name accurately: the immaterial current convergence produces a perceptual background that those with sensitivity find clarifying and those without find simply strange. Administratum staff posted here longest mention, in informal conversation, that they find it increasingly difficult to imagine being posted elsewhere.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Output, Faith, Order (all four, plus Garrison)

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Underbelly
> The orbital complex was not constructed by the Ascendant. It was *found*. The pre-conquest survey found a structure already here — damaged, ancient, partially functional — that the Ascendant's engineers stripped, reinforced, and built over. The original structure's architecture is visible in the Underbelly, the complex's lowest level: corridors that don't connect the way they should, chambers whose dimensions defeat easy mapping, materials not in any conquest-era engineering record.
>
> A1 knows this. Classified at the level where classification functions as a denial of the subject's existence.
>
> The Underbelly is not uninhabited. The Ascendant is aware of this and has been treating the question with the patience of someone who does not want an answer until they understand what they would do with it.
>

## Pale Meridian
*Battle Site / Transit Point · Imperial (nominal)*

The final engagement of the conquest. The debris field is eleven months old — still dense, marked on Guild charts as intermittently mobile in ways that standard physics does not fully explain.

Four inhabited stations at the field's navigable edge house salvage crews, Guild survey teams, and Administratum record-reconstruction teams. The Administratum is reconstructing vessel registrations and hull-mark documentation with particular thoroughness.

**Statistics:** Loyalty

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only
> One Guild survey team has been logging structural anomalies in the debris field that don't originate from any vessel in the engagement record. The material is old — not conquest-era, not Conclave-era. The survey lead's working hypothesis, filed but not forwarded: the material was here *before* the engagement. Before any human presence.
>
> The Administratum vessel-registration reconstruction has found a hull-mark designation belonging to an Ondrak subsidiary. The vessel doesn't appear in either side's engagement records. O1 has been privately notified and is managing it carefully.
>
> Cross-reference: [[Pre-Human Presence Thread]], [[House Ondrak]] (O1).

## Meridian Gate
*Transit Hub · Imperial / Ductus Guild*

The primary approach to the Seam — a natural navigational funnel created by two failed stars. The Guild maintains one of its largest waystations outside the Anchorage here, handling 1,400 vessels simultaneously.

The Guild waystation has been logging *void-current variance* for eight months — navigational readings that don't match the Anchorage's baseline calibration. Three requests for a calibration review team have been filed. D1 has not authorised the review because a review would create a formal record of something D1 is not yet ready to formalise.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Output, Order

## Other Core Systems

**[[Dusk Relay]]** — Central communications hub. 900,000 dedicated staff. Contains a reserved transmission channel with authentication keys predating the current Administratum structure. D1 installed the encryption and retains a read key. Has used it once.

**[[The Pallid Registry]]** — Central archival station for conquest-era land grants and territorial documentation. The head archivist has found an inconsistency in founding documentation underpinning Vethara's territorial claim. The query is sitting with A1.

**[[Covenant Station]]** — Diplomatically neutral waystation used for preliminary Charter negotiations. D1 installed the communications encryption and retained a read key. Used it to access a transmission confirming the forged Vethara precedent.

**[[Vigil Prime]]** — Pre-conquest battle-station, taken intact. Garrison of 800,000 long-service veterans. Three soldiers here have stopped responding to conditioning verification protocols — not dramatically, but the confirmation response is gone. See [[Fraying Conditioning Thread]].

**[[Sonder's Approach]]** — Guild research station studying a gravitational-immaterial convergence zone that has been developing *directionality* over the past eight months. The analyst has not filed this in the formal record.
