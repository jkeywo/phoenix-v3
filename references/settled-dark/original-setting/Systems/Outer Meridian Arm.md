## Valdric Station
*Compact Hub · Valdric Compact (besieged)*

Eleven mining habitats in loose cluster. The Valdric Compact declared independence upon the Conclave's fall — three paragraphs, politically precise, no heroic language — describing six centuries of extraction and asking to be left alone. Their petition for reintegration terms received no response. [[Campaign Iron]] has been besieging for three months.

The Compact's metal production — rare transition metals essential to Guild navigation instruments and military vessel construction — has been at zero since the siege began.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Output

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — Archive Record
> The largest habitat's maintenance archives contain a sensor record filed eight months before the conquest as equipment error. The reading came from a heading consistent with D2's uncharted route. The equipment was not malfunctioning.

## Irongate
*Military Staging Hub · Janissariat*

A former resupply station, now Campaign Iron's staging hub. Six million civilian residents remain, reclassified as essential civilian support personnel. The garrison occupies significant dock capacity. The young people of Irongate are enlisting in the Janissariat at rates the original population interprets in multiple contradictory ways.

Three soldiers currently stationed here were part of the Vethis Secundus breach team at the underground sites.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Order

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Three Soldiers
> The three veterans are functional, professional, and will not discuss Vethis Secundus. Two spend off-hours in a room together, not talking. The third stands at the external observation port during sleep hours watching the void. When asked what they were watching for, the soldier said: *"I'm not sure yet."*
>
> The garrison commander filed three incident reports, reviewed them, and has not forwarded them. The commander is J3's subordinate.
>
> Cross-reference: [[Fraying Conditioning Thread]].

## Guild Anchorage
*Ductus Training Station · Ductus Guild*

Where navigators are made. Candidates spend thirty to seventy years learning to translate what they feel into something ships can follow. The training process pushes sensitivity deliberately. The washout rate is not publicly disclosed.

The station is built to minimise electromagnetic noise. Two million two hundred thousand permanent residents: trainees, active navigators, research staff, support workers, and retired navigators who have nowhere else that feels like home.

**Statistics:** Output, Faith, Order

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — Training Anomalies
> D1 has not shared the changed transmission character with the Anchorage navigators as a group. Several advanced trainees have independently been logging unsolicited impressions during exercises — not consistent with standard current-pattern, directional in a way that geography is not. The training staff have been categorising these as *advanced sensitivity responses* and monitoring them without telling D1.
>
> D2's uncharted route, if followed to its terminus, passes through a void-region the Anchorage's research division flagged as anomalous sixty years ago. The flag is in a filed report that D2 has not read.

## Ashen Reach
*Pacified Territory · Imperial (fragile)*

The conquest here was not clean. The world is called what it is called partly because of the landscape, and partly because of what it looked like for eighteen years after the engagement.

The Ecclesiatum mission's reform-adjacent prelate has been meeting with rural community leaders for discussions that are, technically, about legitimate resistance theology. These are real discussions. They are also, structurally, a network. The garrison commander has been reporting them as standard pastoral activity.

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — Network Contact
> The rural communities' network has been in irregular contact with the parallel governance structure on [[Vethis Secundus]] for approximately eight months. Not organised. Not directed. The contact is through cargo crew who carry messages at informal request. The messages appear domestic — family news, shared practices. Whether that is all they are is harder to determine.

## Other Arm Locations

**[[Blind Shore]]** — Last reliable notation on the Guild's charts. Four million people across six inhabited stations. The community's oral knowledge includes *the heading you don't follow* — a current that feels unusually clear, that two crews have attempted and one did not return from. D2 does not know this is known here.

**[[The Turning]]** — Former Ductus research station, now independent. The most precise outer-arm current charts in the Settled Dark. The senior navigator — a Guild washout — has used the directionality reading to calculate a specific spatial destination. The report has been filed at highest urgency four days ago. No acknowledgement has come.

**[[Wreck Shoal]]** — Salvage community in a dense field of scuttled vessels. The Sorenne Flag has been using the Shoal as a waypoint for eight months. The community leadership is aware. They have not asked questions. This is a considered choice.

**[[The Severance]]** — Former Conclave fortification, stripped and abandoned, now housing 80,000 settlers in genuine legal ambiguity. The defensive grid is intact and potentially restorable. O1 has read the Administratum assessment of this. Ondrak technical staff have visited twice under commercial pretexts.
