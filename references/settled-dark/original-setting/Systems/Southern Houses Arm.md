## Aethon's Forge
*Noble House Throne World · House Aethon*

The system's young star runs hot; the seat world's geology is active. The main settlement occupies a stabilised caldera landscape — the city has grown into the volcano rather than beside it. Augmentation ranges across the population from subtle to extensive. The House's household retainers are unusual in ways that Aethon considers descriptive and other Houses find unsettling.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Output

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — Fraying Conditioning
> Ae2's neurological research has produced a finding not yet shared with anyone: the fraying of Janissariat conditioning in peacetime is not degradation. The conditioning suppresses a perceptual capability, and the fraying is that capability becoming accessible again — without framework or context — in people who spent decades being trained that unexplained perceptions are a security risk.
>
> The three Irongate veterans who served at [[Vethis Secundus]] are on a faster timeline. The path from Ae2's research to this conclusion is visible to anyone with access to both the research and the Vethis operational records. No one currently has both.
>

## Mourne's Watch
*Noble House Throne World · House Mourne*

A high-plateau world with observation towers that began as astronomical survey infrastructure and have accumulated the additional function of architectural philosophy: House Mourne pays attention to long patterns. The towers are the most visible evidence of this.

Significant portion of the population arrived for employment in the House's archive operations — the most rigorous historical pattern-analysis capability of any Noble House.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Order

## New Sorenne
*Contested · House Sorenne civilian remnant*

When the Sorenne Flag scattered the fleet rather than surrender at Pale Meridian, this world received the surviving non-military population of House Sorenne — stripped of their official designation and their institutional home. Population sixty million: farmers, merchants, educators, medical staff. Neither loyal resistance nor pacified territory.

The world is not dangerous. It is watched.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Output

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Supply Chain
> The Flag's supply chain passes through New Sorenne in ways the Janissariat's surveillance has not fully mapped, because the population is not actively supplying the fleet — the fleet passes things *through* civilian cargo networks using intermediaries who don't know the ultimate destination. Several members of the civilian population are doing this independently, without coordination. The surveillance apparatus looking for a network is missing the absence of one.

## Callux Prime
*Neutral World · Unclaimed (contested)*

Three billion people. Three Noble Houses have at various points claimed it; none currently occupies it with sufficient presence to make the claim stick. The population has spent six hundred years developing governance infrastructure sophisticated enough to manage the resulting institutional uncertainty. Currently holds a Charter negotiation seat no one expected it to have.

**Statistics:** Loyalty, Output, Order

## Other Arm Locations

**[[Sorenne's Fall]]** — The system where the Sorenne fleet engaged before Pale Meridian. Forty million people living under the debris field's shadow. The population governs itself through the structure the former colonial administration became when it stopped receiving orders and kept functioning anyway. The Flag has been delivering irregular unregistered supplies for four months.

**[[Mourne's Deep]]** — House Mourne's long-range observation station. Four hundred and fifty years of continuous occupation. The analysis chief has a cosmological observation suggesting something is on a trajectory toward a threshold event in approximately twenty years. The private notes use the phrase *arrival becoming visible*.

**[[The Proving]]** — House Aethon's research station. The ambient perceptual capability in the southern arm's general population has increased by a factor of three over the past eight months. Ae2's team has verified the instruments. The increase is real and they cannot account for it.

**[[Sorenne's Edge]]** — Station occupied by the Sorenne Flag eleven days after the Administratum planted a survey marker and moved on. Population 300,000. The station commander has been receiving supply deliveries that don't match logistics allocations from central command. A parallel logistics operation is being run through this station that is not supposed to appear in the central record.
