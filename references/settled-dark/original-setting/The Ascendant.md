
*Stands alone. No team council. The most powerful individual in the history of the Settled Dark.*

---

The conquest is complete. You are not sure what winning means anymore.

You are using your power to do something genuinely difficult: build an empire that deserves to exist. The [[The Charter of Settlement|Charter of Settlement]] is not a formality. It is the only thing standing between your conquest and a millennium of succession wars.

The [[The Throne Eternal|Throne Eternal]] complicates this. Each Communion clarifies you — something is sorting through you, keeping what it finds useful, setting the rest aside. Your hands work differently than they used to. You have not told anyone. You are not afraid of becoming something better than you are. You are afraid of becoming something whose goodness you cannot verify.

---

## Mechanics
- Conducts a **Tarot Session** with Control each Phase One (representing Communion)
- Has a private **[[Core Mechanics#The Clarity Track|Clarity Track]]** (starts at 4)
- May issue up to **3 Conditional Orders** per round
- Attends the **Diplomacy Council** in Phase Two
- Receives **8 Influence** per round

## Opening Pressures
Three documents require immediate response:
1. A formal [[Ecclesiatum Perpetua]] petition about [[Campaign Pale]]'s methods
2. A [[Ductus Guild]] submission requiring acknowledgement before Charter signature
3. An unsigned note: *"The clause was not in the original draft. I cannot say more at the Seam. Destroy this."*

## Goals
- **Threshold:** Sign the Charter with Ecclesiatum sanctification intact. Ensure at least three [[The Five Pillars|Pillars]] are at 4 or above at the Ceremony.
- **Ambition:** Resolve the Ductus petition, Janissariat constitutional status, and the Succession Clause without any single faction gaining disproportionate Charter advantage.
- **Legacy:** Was the empire you built worth building? Not strategically. *Morally.*

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Transformation
> The Ascendant's hands are structurally different from a human's — the underlying architecture has been reconfigured. [[Ae1]] observed this in a private audience and found it beautiful. The Ascendant is not becoming something monstrous. They may be becoming something that cannot verify its own goodness.
>
> [[Ae3]]'s historical research on previous apotheosis-adjacent figures is relevant: none of them died. The accounts describe *departure* — the subject simply was no longer there in any conventional sense, at a certain point in the process.
>
> The Clarity Track is the mechanical expression of this. At Clarity 0, Control and the player determine together what happens. It will not be quiet.
