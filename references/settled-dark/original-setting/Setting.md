
## The Settled Dark

The **Settled Dark** is the name given to the roughly two hundred inhabited worlds, stations, and void-habitats where humanity has, against considerable odds, survived. They cling to the light of their suns like candles in a cathedral — brave, small, temporary.

For three millennia the Settled Dark was governed — loosely, contentiously, brutally — by the **Conclave of Thrones**: a council of the six great dynastic Houses who had carved the viable worlds into personal fiefdoms. It was corrupt, cruel, and calcified. Most people born in the Settled Dark died in the same conditions as their grandparents.

The Conclave is gone. The Ascendant saw to that.

## The Void

Between the inhabited worlds lies the void — not empty, but full of things that predate human ambition and will outlast it. Ships cross it in days rather than generations only because of the **[[The Ductus Guild|Ductus Guild]]**: psychic navigators who *feel* the currents of the **immaterial deep** and carry civilisation on their backs.

The immaterial deep is not fully understood. The [[Ductus Guild]] has built its authority on a relationship with it for three thousand years. That relationship has recently changed.

## The Conquest

Sixty years ago, the [[The Ascendant|Ascendant]] broke the Conclave, world by world. The [[Janissariat High Command|Janissariat]] — three billion psychically conditioned warriors — made it possible. Eleven months ago, the last Conclave loyalist fleet broke at the [[Pale Meridian|Battle of Pale Meridian]]. The conquest is complete.

The peace has not begun.

## The Throne Eternal

The [[The Throne Eternal|Throne Eternal]] is the [[Ecclesiatum Perpetua|Ecclesiatum's]] name for the foundational moral principle of the universe — something very old, not quite a god, more like a direction. A weight in the moral fabric of reality that, when ignored long enough, produces consequences. The Ecclesiatum was built to transmit its teachings. Whether it has done so honestly is a question some are beginning to ask.
