
The **Throne Eternal** is the foundational spiritual concept of the [[Ecclesiatum Perpetua]]. It is not quite a god. It is more like a direction — a weight in the moral fabric of reality that corrects civilisations that have lost their way.

## What the Ecclesiatum Says
The Ecclesiatum describes it as a discovered principle: something real, ancient, and *directional*. It moves toward something. The institution's founders encountered it and chose to name it and build a framework around it. The Ecclesiatum's purpose is to transmit its teachings and to sanctify governance that aligns with its will.

The [[The Ascendant|Ascendant]] is officially the **Instrument of the Throne Eternal** — a being chosen to correct a corrupt civilisation.

## What the Founding Texts Actually Say

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The True Record
> The Ecclesiatum's founding texts — not the public canon, but originals held in the central archive — do not describe the Throne Eternal as a revelation. They describe it as a **discovery**: something already present, unnamed and very old, which the founders encountered and chose to institutionalise.
>
> The texts do not describe it as benevolent. They describe it as **directional**. It moves toward something. The founders did not know what.
>
> Separately, [[House Mourne|Mourne]]'s [[M2|Faith Scholar]] has found pre-Conclave colonial correspondence describing the Throne Eternal as something *encountered* by colonial populations — a phenomenon the early Ecclesiatum was deliberately constructed to provide a managed framework for. See [[Fraying Conditioning Thread]] and [[Void Integrity Thread]] for convergent evidence.
>
> The community records at [[Orreval]] describe the same phenomenon independently, calling it *the attending* — a quality of presence at that world that is directional. Not localised. Pointing somewhere.

## Relationship to the Immaterial Deep
The [[Ductus Guild]]'s navigation ability is reception — the Guild's navigators feel the currents because something in the immaterial deep is transmitting. The transmission has changed character over the past eighteen years. See [[Void Integrity Thread]].
