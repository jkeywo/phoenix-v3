
# House Mourne — The Reckonists

*"The account is always kept. The only question is when it is settled."*

---

Every action accumulates debt. The universe maintains its ledgers with infinite patience. The Conclave fell because its debt became unpayable. House Mourne does not seek to prevent reckoning; they seek to understand its shape and be correctly positioned for what follows.

They are quiet at court. Observational. They take very careful notes. Other Houses find this morbid. House Mourne finds other Houses naive.

House Mourne's seat is [[Mourne's Watch]]. Their long-range observation station at [[Mourne's Deep]] maintains the longest continuously accumulated outer-void data set in the Settled Dark.

---

## Characters

### M1 — War Analyst
Presence at the War Council is observational — understand the military situation accurately so the House is positioned correctly when it changes. Has been running figures on Janissariat Readiness trajectory.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — M1
> Your calculation: without a significant change, Readiness will hit critical threshold within two game rounds. You have been positioning for the aftermath.
>
> What your calculation does not account for: whether two rounds is actually correct. M3 has said nothing to suggest urgency. You have not asked directly.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Accurately map the Janissariat's Readiness trajectory. Position Mourne for the aftermath.
> - *Ambition:* Establish at least one security relationship with a non-Janissariat military actor before Readiness hits critical.
> - *Legacy:* You predicted the collapse. Did predicting it make it more likely?

### M2 — Faith Scholar
Studies the Ecclesiatum historically, as a system whose persistence tells something about the specific problem it was built to solve.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — M2
> Mourne's archive contains pre-Conclave colonial correspondence describing the Throne Eternal as something *encountered* by populations — a phenomenon the early Ecclesiatum was deliberately constructed to manage. The Throne Eternal was not discovered. It was named, shaped, and institutionalised as a management tool for a population that had started hearing something it did not have vocabulary for. The correspondence's description of what happens when the managed framework fails is alarming enough that you copied it by hand and carry it with you.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Keep Mourne's Faith Council presence informational — gather intelligence on the fracture without committing to a position.
> - *Ambition:* Find someone else at the Seam who understands the Throne Eternal's actual nature. Determine whether what they know is compatible with what you know.
> - *Legacy:* You hold the founding documents. What are you going to do with them?

### M3 — Void Analyst
Applies Mourne's historical pattern analysis to current political data. The model says the reckoning is not months away — it is weeks, possibly less.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — M3
> The primary driver is not Readiness alone but a compound: Charter negotiation failure probability, Ecclesiatum fracture acceleration, and a set of void-trade anomalies in fringe data that your model flags as a stress indicator without being able to specify the cause.
>
> You have not told M1 the timeline is wrong. Telling M1 requires explaining the void-trade anomaly component, which you cannot yet account for.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Refine the collapse timeline to a precision allowing Mourne to act rather than merely observe.
> - *Ambition:* Identify the void-trade anomaly component. Understand what is feeding the stress calculation before the Ceremony.
> - *Legacy:* The numbers were right. Did being right help anyone?

---

## Faction Goals
- **Threshold:** Correctly map the collapse timeline. Protect Mourne's assets and influence.
- **Ambition:** Understand the void anomaly component. Position for the aftermath of at least one major crisis.
- **Legacy:** Mourne knows the reckoning is coming. Did they use that knowledge wisely?

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Analysis
> M3's model has a variable it cannot account for. The void-trade anomalies feeding into the collapse calculation are sourced from fringe trade data being monitored through commercial channels the Ductus Guild does not know are being watched. If the Guild discovers this, the data access ends. If M3 asks directly, the relationship changes.
>
> [[Mourne's Deep]]'s analysis chief has a cosmological observation suggesting something is on a trajectory toward a threshold event in approximately twenty years. M3 has the data. M3 does not have the private notes with the interpretation: *arrival becoming visible.*
