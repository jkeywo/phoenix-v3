
# House Aethon — The Becomers

*"What was born is not what can be. The self is a draft, not a declaration."*

---

Through experience, augmentation, choice, and deliberate transcendence, the self is remade. House Aethon are the most extensively augmented Noble House and the most genuinely difficult to read. They regard the [[The Ascendant|Ascendant]] not primarily as a ruler but as a *phenomenon* — a human being visibly in the process of becoming something else.

House Aethon's seat is [[Aethon's Forge]]. Their research station at [[The Proving]] runs the most advanced private neurological research programme in the Settled Dark.

---

## Characters

### Ae1 — Court Delegate
In a private audience with the Ascendant four months ago, watched with the specific quality of attention House Aethon cultivates. The Ascendant's hands were wrong — not damaged, but reconfigured. Ae1 found it beautiful. Still finds it beautiful. Believes the observation was reciprocal.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — Ae1
> Ae2 wants the Ascendant as a research subject. You have the relationship that would make that access possible. You have been reluctant to broker it because what you observed in that audience felt reciprocal — the Ascendant was watching you with the same quality of attention you were watching them with. You are not sure what they were measuring.
>
> You have also been investigating the Janissariat's garrison command irregularity at [[Vethis Prime]] and found three other garrison command irregularities in the southern arm, all introduced during the same administrative period. Mapped, they form a strategic pattern. You have told no one.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Maintain Aethon's Diplomacy Council relationships. Advance formal recognition of augmentation rights in the Charter.
> - *Ambition:* Secure a second private audience with the Ascendant. Determine whether the transformation is accelerating and who is directing it.
> - *Legacy:* You observed something extraordinary and protected it from being studied. Was that reverence or possessiveness?

### Ae2 — Research Director
Runs Aethon's augmentation research. Systematic study of perceptual capabilities across populations with and without augmentation, specifically investigating the relationship between augmentation and the class of sensitivity the Ductus Guild cultivates in navigators. Running for forty years.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — Ae2
> Three weeks ago, a dataset analysis produced a finding you have not yet shared with anyone, including yourself: the ambient perceptual capability in the southern arm's general population has increased by a factor of three over the past eight months. The instruments are correct. The team verified them. The increase is real, consistent across all measurement sites, and does not correspond to any controlled variable. It is happening to people who have no relationship to augmentation or Guild training.
>
> Your team's working hypothesis: something in the deep void is affecting the population's perceptual baseline at a level too subtle to notice in daily experience but clearly visible in systematic measurement.
>
> You want access to the Ascendant as a research subject. Ae1 has the relationship and is withholding it for reasons you find professionally frustrating and partially understand.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Advance augmentation rights clauses far enough to protect Aethon's research programme from post-Charter review.
> - *Ambition:* Obtain documented observation of the Ascendant's transformation.
> - *Legacy:* The research is real and the findings are significant. Who do they belong to?

### Ae3 — Void Scholar
Has spent thirty years compiling accounts of individuals who underwent experiences analogous to the Ascendant's. None of them died. The accounts describe departure.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — Ae3
> Every account describes the same thing: at a certain point in the process, the subject was no longer there in any conventional sense. Not gone violently or in crisis. *Left* — in a way witnesses recorded with the vocabulary of people describing something they had no framework for.
>
> You find this hopeful. You are aware you may be the only person at the Seam who would.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Complete your comparative historical analysis — identify whether the Ascendant's current state corresponds to any documented phase in the precedent accounts.
> - *Ambition:* Share your findings with the Ascendant directly. Give them the only honest account of what the historical record says happens next.
> - *Legacy:* The others left. If the Ascendant leaves, what does the empire do? Did you think about that part?

## Faction Goals
- **Threshold:** Augmentation rights protected in Charter. Ascendant's transformation documented.
- **Ambition:** Research programme secured. Historical precedent delivered to the Ascendant.
- **Legacy:** House Aethon studies becoming. Did they help the Ascendant become something good?

The research gap that would allow someone to connect Ae2's findings with J3's Vethis operational records remains unclosed. No one currently has access to both.
