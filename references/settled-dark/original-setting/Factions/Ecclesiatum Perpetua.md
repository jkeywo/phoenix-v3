*Three thousand years old. Fractured in a way that cannot be hidden much longer.*

---

The Ecclesiatum came to the Seam to sanctify the [[The Charter of Settlement|Charter]]. Whether it will leave as a unified institution is a different question. The fracture runs along three lines:

- **Orthodox hierarchy** — sanctified the conquest; will sanctify the Charter on the same grounds
- **Reformist current** — an Ecclesiatum that merely confirms Imperial authority has no independent spiritual legitimacy
- **Third current** — small, careful, whispered about — believes the Ascendant has become the corruption the Throne Eternal was supposed to correct

The Ecclesiatum holds an **absolute veto** over the Charter's execution: if it withholds sanctification, the Charter does not take effect.

---

## Characters

### E1 — High Prelate
Will perform the sanctification. Has stewarded the Ecclesiatum for three hundred years. The Throne Eternal's direction is, as best E1 can determine, toward the survival and flourishing of humanity. E1 has committed to this reading. On most days is certain it is correct.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — E1
> The Ecclesiatum's founding texts do not describe the Throne Eternal as a revelation. They describe it as a discovery — something already present, unnamed and very old. The texts describe it as *directional*. It moves toward something. The founders did not know what. Neither do you.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Perform the sanctification. Keep the Ecclesiatum publicly unified through the Ceremony.
> - *Ambition:* Secure the Institutional Authority Clause in a form giving genuine independence without rupturing the Ascendant relationship.
> - *Legacy:* Is the institution you've steered for three hundred years pointed in a direction worth following?

### E2 — Reformist Prelate
Loves the Ecclesiatum the way you love something you are afraid is destroying itself. Three thousand years of spiritual authority, and it spent sixty years sanctifying a conquest because the hierarchy lacked the courage to name what it was. The sanctification is the only leverage E2 has, and E2 intends to use it.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — E2
> The reformist movement's funding has not come entirely from within the Ecclesiatum. [[House Vethara]] has contributed through indirect channels for forty years. You have been careful not to ask whether they believe in the cause or are purchasing leverage. You are afraid of the answer.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Secure a meaningful amendment to the Institutional Authority Clause before sanctification.
> - *Ambition:* Establish the Ecclesiatum as genuinely independent in the Charter's final text.
> - *Legacy:* Did you reform the institution, or fracture it in the attempt?
>
> [!danger]- 🔒 Conspiracy — Cell One
> You are Cell One's Ecclesiatum representative. You believe the sacred charter Cell One is working toward is genuine institutional independence achieved through constitutional means. What you have not fully examined is whether the specific language Cell One wants would restore the Ecclesiatum's authority or merely transfer dependence from the Ascendant to whoever controls the charter's interpretation.
>
> See [[The Conspiracy]] for full cell brief.

### E3 — Diplomatic Legate
Reform-adjacent in sympathies but trained by the Diplomacy Council into pragmatism. E2 has leverage; they do not have enough allies. Has studied the Ecclesiatum's constitutional history carefully enough to know what the sacred charter language Cell One is proposing would actually produce.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — E3
> The ancient texts describe concentrated spiritual interpretive authority as dangerous — not politically dangerous, but in a harder-to-categorise sense, as though the consolidation creates something the Throne Eternal responds to. They do not describe the response as positive.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Maintain Diplomacy Council relationships without fracturing the faction.
> - *Ambition:* Build a coalition giving the Ecclesiatum genuine Charter leverage independent of the sanctification threat.
> - *Legacy:* Did you serve the institution, or protect it from itself?
>
> [!danger]- 🔒 Conspiracy — Cell One
> You are in Cell One. You believe in the goal. You are beginning to believe the method is wrong, and you do not yet know what to do with this.
>
> See [[The Conspiracy]] for full cell brief.

### E4 — Inquiry Prelate
Opened the [[Campaign Pale]] inquiry without authorisation because the operational reports were wrong in a specific way — not falsified, but structured to describe only what the reporting officer decided to include. The absences were the finding. Has found traces of what happened at the [[Vethis Secundus]] underground sites. Has told no one.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — E4
> The full documented findings: the underground sites predate human settlement by an interval the geological survey declined to specify. They showed signs of recent use — not by the local population. Three soldiers are listed KIA. Four veterans gave accounts the inquiry cannot publish and that you have read three times and are still processing.
>
> You know someone else at the Seam has seen what those veterans described. You have been watching back.
>
> E1 needs the inquiry controlled. Closing it now would require destroying the documentation, and you cannot do that to the record and still be the person you believe yourself to be.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Keep the inquiry's most significant findings out of the Charter process without suppressing them permanently.
> - *Ambition:* Find the person at the Seam who has seen what the veterans described. Share what you know.
> - *Legacy:* Was opening this inquiry the right thing to do, regardless of what it costs the Ecclesiatum?
