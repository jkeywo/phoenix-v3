
# House Vethara — The Legitimists

*"What was built before you must be understood before you may change it."*

---

Authority derives from continuity. Bloodline, precedent, and accumulated history are the only honest sources of power. House Vethara holds the pre-conquest legal record — the Ascendant needed the conquest to look like restoration rather than replacement, and Vethara had the documents. Their survival was the price of their cooperation. Both parties understood the transaction clearly.

House Vethara's power base is [[Tessavar]] and the [[Vethara Seat]]. They hold ceremonial authorities required for the Charter signing to be constitutionally valid under the oldest traditions.

---

## Characters

### V1 — House Speaker
Architect of Vethara's Charter strategy: ancestral property rights restored in legally precise language, and the Ecclesiatum's Institutional Authority Clause grounded in historical precedent rather than Imperial grant.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — V1
> You have been quietly funding the Ecclesiatum's reformist current for forty years. You genuinely believe in Ecclesiatum independence. You also believe an independent Ecclesiatum is structurally better for Vethara than a dependent one.
>
> What is keeping you awake: one of the precedents underpinning Vethara's ancestral property claims does not hold. It is wrong in a specific documentary way that suggests it was constructed rather than inherited — a five-hundred-year-old forgery with an Administratum archive seal on it. If challenged during negotiations, the property claims collapse. You are managing this alone.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Secure ancestral property rights restoration in the Charter. Support the Institutional Authority Clause amendment.
> - *Ambition:* Identify the source of the forged precedent without exposing its existence.
> - *Legacy:* The House you are protecting — does it deserve the protection?

### V2 — Ceremonial Warden
Holds the pre-conquest ceremonial authorities required for valid Charter execution. Three weeks ago was approached by someone acting for [[The Conspiracy|Cell One]] and accepted an offer.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — V2
> In exchange for facilitating Cell One's preferred Institutional Authority Clause language, you were promised guarantee that the restored property rights use language resolving an ambiguity in Vethara's favour — an ambiguity V1 has not mentioned to you. You accepted. You have not told V1 or V3.
>
> V1's strategy and Cell One's strategy are aligned broadly but not in detail, and you have made a commitment that may require you to support Charter language V1 would not choose.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Perform Vethara's ceremonial functions. Maintain value to Cell One without exposing the arrangement to V1.
> - *Ambition:* Ensure the property rights language delivers what you were promised.
> - *Legacy:* You traded something that wasn't yours to trade. What did you get for it?
>
> [!danger]- 🔒 Conspiracy — Cell One
> You are Cell One's Vethara representative. Your ceremonial authority is the practical mechanism through which the sacred charter becomes constitutionally valid. You believe in the goal. You are not certain you believe in everyone pursuing it.
>
> See [[The Conspiracy]] for full cell brief.

### V3 — Claims Advocate
Manages the documentation and legal argument for Vethara's individual ancestral property claims. Discovered last night, after working through the full Charter draft, that V1's preferred Institutional Authority Clause language conflicts with V3's largest property claim.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — V3
> The conflict is technical and buried in cross-reference: the succession reading that makes V3's largest claim valid requires an interpretation of ancestral right that V1's preferred Charter language would, in two clauses, implicitly foreclose. V1 either doesn't know or has made a calculation you're not aware of. You have not slept.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Secure legal validation for Vethara's three largest property claims.
> - *Ambition:* Resolve the conflict between your claims language and V1's preferred clause text.
> - *Legacy:* The claims are documented. The House gets what it was owed. Is that enough?

## Faction Goals
- **Threshold:** Ancestral property rights restored. Ceremonial authorities performed.
- **Ambition:** Institutional Authority Clause amended favourably. Forged precedent contained.
- **Legacy:** Legitimacy built on a five-hundred-year-old forgery — is the House legitimate?

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Forged Precedent
> The forgery is genuine and five hundred years old. The [[Pallid Registry]]'s head archivist has found the inconsistency through standard verification procedure and submitted a query to the Registry's legal analysis wing. A1 has the query and is holding it.
>
> V2's survey commission has found structural remains in the outer administrative zone — the same category as those at [[Pale Meridian]] and the [[The Seam|Seam's Underbelly]]. V2 has told no one, including V1. V2 logged them as geological formations.
