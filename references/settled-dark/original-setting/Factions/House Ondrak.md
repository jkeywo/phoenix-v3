House Ondrak — The Realists

*"Power is the only currency that cannot be counterfeited."*

---

Everything else — faith, law, tradition — is a story told by those who hold power to justify having taken it. House Ondrak does not despise these things; they understand them as useful tools. Their loyalty is transactional and they see nothing shameful in this. They supplied troops to both sides during the conquest. Both sides knew. No one was surprised.

House Ondrak's seat is [[Ondrak's Reach]]. Their household forces are widely considered the best-trained private military in the surviving Houses.

---

## Characters

### O1 — Strategic Liaison
Six weeks ago concluded a private military compact with a Janissariat officer below High Command level — mutual security guarantee in exchange for a post-Charter commitment regarding Ondrak's property security. The kind of compact the Charter's drafting process is specifically designed to prohibit.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — O1
> If the Janissariat constitutional status clause fails, the officer has no authority to honour it. You need the clause to succeed, or the arrangement to stay undisclosed, or both.
>
> What you have not told O2 or O3: the private compact's underpinning operation — from thirty years ago — is the same one that put the Ondrak subsidiary's name on a vessel registration in the [[Pale Meridian]] debris field. The Administratum survey team is reconstructing registrations. You are managing this carefully.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Keep the War Council's resourcing decisions from exposing the private compact.
> - *Ambition:* Secure a durable post-Charter military relationship not dependent on a single officer's discretion.
> - *Legacy:* You bought security with the House's resources. Was the price honest?

### O2 — Trade Envoy
Conducting commercial negotiations with [[House Caelifen]] that have progressed well beyond initial scope — what began as a joint venture has evolved into something that functions as a practical merger of commercial interests.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — O2
> The most recent agreement contains clauses that would, if Caelifen invoked them, give that House significant influence over Ondrak's commercial decisions. You negotiated them because the deal required them. You have not told O1 or O3 the extent of the entanglement.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Advance Ondrak's commercial interests at the Commerce Council without disclosing the entanglement.
> - *Ambition:* Convert the Caelifen relationship into a formal alliance on terms Ondrak controls.
> - *Legacy:* You made the deal. Did you read all of it?

### O3 — Court Broker
Works the Diplomacy Council. Has been holding intelligence for three weeks: the Ascendant has been making private resource allocations to an undisclosed project — something being constructed, not appearing on any Administratum record.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — O3
> The allocations are significant. The Administratum's official records have been managed to omit them deliberately. Used at the right moment, this information reshapes a negotiation. Used too late, it becomes common knowledge and loses value entirely.
>
> O3's audit will also, within thirty days, find a contract at [[Orvath]] containing clauses that give [[House Caelifen]] acquisition rights over Ondrak commercial assets under specific trigger conditions. O3 will need to tell O1 and O2, neither of whom knows this contract exists.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Maintain Diplomacy Council relationships. Gather at least two pieces of actionable intelligence.
> - *Ambition:* Deploy the Ascendant's undisclosed project information at maximum political value.
> - *Legacy:* You held the information. Was waiting the right call?

## Faction Goals
- **Threshold:** Property interests protected in the Charter. Military compact undisclosed.
- **Ambition:** Caelifen entanglement converted to Ondrak-controlled alliance.
- **Legacy:** The realist position — did it produce stability or just a deferred reckoning?

>
> The commander was transferred to Pale Hook eight months ago — the same time the defensive systems were updated — through a command authority channel O1 has formal authority to use but has not used for a deployment of this scope before.
