
*Without the Ductus, there is no empire. They know this. They are waiting on a petition filed fourteen years ago.*

---

Every ship that moves, every supply line, every military campaign depends on Guild navigators feeling the currents of the immaterial deep. The Guild does not need to threaten anyone. The structural reality does the work.

The navigation ability is not innate talent. It is **reception**. Something in the immaterial deep is transmitting, and the Guild has spent three millennia developing the sensitivity to receive. The transmission has changed. The three representatives at the Seam each know different parts of what the change means. They have not yet compared notes.

The Guild receives **2 Influence** per round but derives significant wealth from its Marks income via open void lanes.

---

## Characters

### D1 — First Navigator
Has been navigating for forty-six years. Eighteen years ago, the transmissions changed character: they stopped feeling like geography and started feeling like instructions. Not in language — nothing that specific. Directional in a way that geography is not. Is following the suggestions selectively. The routes produced have been reliable. This is not reassuring.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — D1
> You have read the Sonder's Approach analyst's private log through an access pathway that would be difficult to explain. You know the convergence there has developed directionality in the past eight months.
>
> You have also installed the encryption infrastructure for [[Covenant Station]]'s diplomatic channels and retained a read key. You have read a transmission confirming that a specific legal precedent in the Charter draft was constructed from forged historical documentation. You have not acted on this.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Secure a formal response to the Guild's navigation rights petition before the Ceremony.
> - *Ambition:* Find another Guild navigator who has noticed the change in transmission character. Compare what you each have received.
> - *Legacy:* The Guild built its authority on a relationship it has never fully understood. Was that honest?

### D2 — Cartographer
Six months ago began charting a route that does not appear on any existing record. It presented itself during a standard exercise. Four ships have used it on Guild-sanctioned expeditions. None of them have returned from the terminus.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — D2
> The ships are not overdue. They have not sent distress signals. They have simply not come back.
>
> The terminus, when you navigate near that direction, feels like *arrival*. Not danger. Not warning. Like something at the end of that route has been expecting visitors and does not find the wait unreasonable.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Secure the Guild's Charter position without disclosing the route to parties who would immediately deploy military assets toward it.
> - *Ambition:* Determine what the terminus is. Find someone trustworthy enough to tell.
> - *Legacy:* You charted a route to something unknown and sent four ships toward it. What did you owe those crews?

### D3 — Trade Liaison
Handles the Guild's commercial relationships. Understands that the navigation rights petition is cover for a second, larger conversation: the question of who is authorised to communicate with whatever is in the immaterial deep. D3 learned about the communication layer through commercial records, not direct reception.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — D3
> You want to establish the Guild as the only licensed intermediary between the empire and the void's communications — not a monopoly on navigation, but a monopoly on something that is not supposed to exist.
>
> You are not certain D1 and D2 know about the communication layer in the same way you do. If they are receiving the transmissions directly, their reaction to your proposal might not be the support you are counting on.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Secure formal response to the petition including language about exclusive Guild navigation authority.
> - *Ambition:* Establish the Guild's communication intermediary role in the Charter through the Commerce or Diplomacy Council.
> - *Legacy:* The Guild has been managing something the empire didn't know about for three millennia. Was that stewardship or concealment?
>
> [!danger]- 🔒 Conspiracy — Cell Two (possible)
> *If assigned a conspiracy slot:* You are in Cell Two. The constitutional council Cell Two is building is, from your perspective, a governance structure within which the Guild's navigation monopoly becomes the most stable form of political leverage. You find this structurally elegant.
>

## Faction Goals
- **Threshold:** Formal response to the navigation rights petition; Charter signature confirmed.
- **Ambition:** Communication intermediary role established constitutionally.
- **Legacy:** Three millennia of managing the void's relationship with humanity. Was it done honestly?
