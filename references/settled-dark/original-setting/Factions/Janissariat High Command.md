*Three billion warriors who broke the Conclave world by world. The conquest is done. Purpose and doctrine are no longer aligned.*

---

The Janissariat's psychic conditioning was designed for war. In peacetime, it frays. Not catastrophically — not yet. Warriors who spent decades in heightened psychic readiness are slowly becoming something more like ordinary people, and ordinary people ask questions that conditioning used to suppress.

The constitutional status clause in the [[The Charter of Settlement|Charter]] is not an abstraction. It is a maintenance problem dressed in legal language. A **sacred order** with autonomous doctrine cannot be stood down. An **Imperial instrument** can. The High Command is not unified on which it prefers.

**[[Core Mechanics#The Readiness Track|Readiness]]** opens at 5. Three campaigns are active. The Charter requires a position.

---

## Characters

### J1 — Marshal-Commander
Built the Janissariat's command doctrine. Believes the sacred order designation is the only viable institutional structure — not a power grab, but the only structure that keeps the conditioning from fraying catastrophically. Has filed three reports about soldiers reporting unexplained perceptions; classified them as operational stress.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — J1
> The conditioning was derived from early Ductus research — the same sensitivity to the immaterial deep, militarised and directed inward. In peacetime, as it frays, some veterans are beginning to receive things. You know this and are concealing it.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Secure Janissariat constitutional status as a sacred order. Maintain Readiness at 4+.
> - *Ambition:* Resolve at least one Fringe Campaign with formal recognition of Janissariat conduct.
> - *Legacy:* Is the institution you built something that will outlast you? Does it deserve to?

### J2 — Strategic Commander
Not a true believer. Views the constitutional status proposal as a sarcophagus — a sacred order cannot be reformed from outside. Wants the Janissariat classified as an Imperial instrument *because* subordinate institutions can be fixed. In direct conflict with J1; neither has said so plainly.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — J2
> You have run the fraying figures. Veterans who lose their conditioning do not become peaceful. The suppression lifted from someone who spent decades in war does not produce a civilian. J1's position leads to an institution that can only be broken or venerated.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Maintain Readiness at 3+. Keep all three campaigns resourced.
> - *Ambition:* Establish a formal veteran transition protocol in the Charter — not disbandment, not preservation. Something new.
> - *Legacy:* Did you plan for the people, or for the institution?

### J3 — Campaign Commander
Commanded the ground operation on [[Vethis Secundus]]. Gave the order to breach the underground sites. Watched what came out. The official report is accurate the way a map is accurate.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — J3
> Three of your soldiers went into it and did not return. You listed them as killed in action. You are watching faces during the Campaign Pale inquiry for someone concealing the same thing you are concealing.
>
> The inquiry must not reach the Charter. That is your operational priority.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Prevent the Campaign Pale inquiry from becoming a Charter issue.
> - *Ambition:* Find the person at the Seam who has seen what you have seen. Share what you know.
> - *Legacy:* The sites are destroyed. Is that better or worse than leaving them intact?

### J4 — Diplomatic Marshal
Represents the Janissariat at the Diplomacy Council. Has been conducting quiet conversations about a compromise on the constitutional status clause that J1 has not sanctioned, because J4 believes J1's position is a negotiating stance mistaken for a bottom line.

> [!note]- 🔒 Private Brief — J4
> Your loyalty is to the Janissariat's survival, not J1's preferred outcome. The conversations have progressed further than J1 knows.
>
> **Goals:**
> - *Threshold:* Secure constitutional protection in whatever form survives negotiation.
> - *Ambition:* Build durable Diplomacy Council relationships independent of Readiness levels.
> - *Legacy:* Was your pragmatism wisdom, or was it surrender dressed as strategy?

## Faction Goals
- **Threshold:** Keep Readiness at 3+. Secure constitutional status in the Charter.
- **Ambition:** Resolve a Fringe Campaign. Establish veteran transition protocol.
- **Legacy:** Is the institution built for the people who serve in it?

> [!warning]- 🔒 GM Only — The Fraying
> The conditioning suppresses a perceptual capability — the same one the Ductus demonstrate in navigation. The fraying is that capability becoming accessible again in people who have been suppressing it for decades.
>
> Three veterans from the [[Vethis Secundus]] underground breach are stationed at [[Irongate]] on J3's personal rotation order. Their behaviour has been logged in three incident reports that stopped at the garrison commander's desk. Two spend off-hours in a room together, not talking. One stands at the external observation port during sleep hours, watching the void.
>
> Additionally, three soldiers at [[Vigil Prime]] have stopped responding to standard verification protocols. Not dramatically — they pass routine evaluations — but the conditioning's confirmation response is gone. One, asked what they were watching for at the observation port, said: *"I keep thinking I can hear something. I'm trying to work out if it's getting louder."*
>
> [[Ae2]]'s research has found the mechanism. No one currently has access to both the research and the operational records. See [[Fraying Conditioning Thread]].
