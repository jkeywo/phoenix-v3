## Goal Structure
Every faction has three tiers of goal:
- **Threshold** — Achievable by most factions playing competently
- **Ambition** — Requires skill or sacrifice from others
- **Legacy** — One per faction. Qualitative. What did your faction's existence *mean*?

See individual faction pages for details.
