# Portrait terminal revision

The terminal was edited with built-in imagegen from `crt-terminal.png`. Prompt: preserve the gritty British sci-fi comic artwork, cream and worn orange casing, knobs, overhead mount and cables; redraw the blank CRT as a taller, narrower portrait display with approximately the same screen area; flat front view, no face, text or QR, transparent exterior.

The resulting image is 1122 by 1402 pixels. `menu-scene.json` records its new aperture and presentation width. The displayed aperture area is within 1% of the preceding landscape screen. Portraits fill the aperture above the name strip; QR codes fit the largest square above the join-code strip, preserving their quiet zone.

`portrait-1.png` through `portrait-12.png` are 192-pixel display copies of the approved naval crew artwork. The original 48-pixel `avatar-*` files remain the network/profile representation. Exact matching of these built-in avatars selects the larger local portrait; custom uploads remain supported.

Native and all web roles built. The two-region native smoke passed, including independent profile editing and readiness. Browser desktop rendering and the native QR/profile layout were visually inspected. Phone-camera QR scanning remains a device check.
