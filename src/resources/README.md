# Resources

Read `stocks/` for finite quantities, resource catalogues and atomic transactions; `networks/` for power/coolant allocation and thermal behaviour; `processors/` for recipes and native work accounting; then `equipment/` for the authoritative fitted-System adapter.

Generic definitions and execution have distinct targets. Content compilation resolves authored references using definition targets without linking authoritative execution. World adapters prepare demands and inspect grants, then commit the same frozen allocation once.

Stock identity and ownership are shared across magazines, material stores, energy and coolant. Effects and processes use those physical owners and exact receipts; they do not introduce another wallet or allocation policy. Preserve deterministic ordering, finite commitments and continuation remainders.
