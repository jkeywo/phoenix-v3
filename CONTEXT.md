# Phoenix domain glossary

- **Controller**: a Console-owned decision maker with one command section and one effective human or AI operator. Physical Systems consume its admitted commands.
- **Controller tree bindings**: the compiled association between a Controller tree's named observations/actions and their permitted sources and destinations. Derived from authored content and rebuilt on load; not saved runtime state.
- **Console control eligibility**: whether a proposed human command is supported by the Console's current permitted observations and resolved layout. Shared by the Console model and remote client; eligibility does not replace authoritative admission at the host.
- **Workshop draft**: editable authored definitions and assets composed with declared dependency packs. A draft can be incomplete while being edited; verification and export require a valid complete pack.
- **Workshop draft resolution**: queries over the current draft and dependencies that supply authoring choices and previews without granting the draft verified status.
